About The Scholars Hub • Faridkot Est. 2023 • Circular Road

A quiet sanctuary
engineered for deep work.

Founded to eliminate power cuts, domestic interruptions, and ambient noise for Faridkot's serious competitive exam candidates.

Preparing for high-stakes competitive examinations like UPSC, NEET, JEE, Banking, or Punjab Civil Services requires marathon hours of deep, uninterrupted revision. Yet studying at home often means dealing with unpredictable domestic noise, unannounced visitors, household errands, and disruptive summer power cuts that break your concentration.

We built The Scholars Hub on Circular Road, Faridkot to provide a professional, uncompromising alternative: an air-conditioned silent library where every detail is optimized for intense cognitive work. Sound-absorbing carpeting, personal high-walled cubicles, stable commercial fiber internet, and full dual-inverter power backup ensure you never lose a minute of your study session.

"In competitive exams, knowledge is half the battle; the other half is the discipline and focus of your study environment."
— The Scholars Hub Founding Principle

When you enter our study hall, you are surrounded by fellow aspirants working toward their own ambitions. There is no conversation, no mobile chatter, and no distraction — only the quiet, energizing hum of collective dedication.

Guiding Standards

The three pillars of our space.

01 / DISCIPLINE

Zero-Distraction Culture

A strictly monitored no-talking policy inside the study halls ensures your concentration remains unbroken through morning, evening, or all-day study shifts.

02 / ERGONOMICS

Physical Comfort for Long Hours

Breathable orthopedic mesh chairs with adjustable lumbar support, paired with non-flicker 4000K lighting designed specifically to avoid eye fatigue and spinal strain.

03 / MOMENTUM

Community of Achievers

Surround yourself with disciplined candidates preparing for civil services, medicine, engineering, and banking whose consistent routine inspires you every single day.

Library Conduct Charter

  • 1
    Silent Mode at All Times: Mobile phones must be set to silent or flight mode upon entering the premises. Calls must be attended in the outer lobby.
  • 2
    Headphone Discipline: Audio from lecture videos, tests, and music must be kept at a volume that does not leak to neighboring desks.
  • 3
    Cafeteria-Only Dining: Meals, snacks, and aromatic beverages must be consumed exclusively in the separated cafeteria break room.
  • 4
    Mutual Respect & Quiet Movement: Please walk quietly, avoid scraping chairs, and respect the deep work focus of fellow scholars.
